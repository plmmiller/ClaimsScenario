## Scene Description

The intersection of Oak Street and Elm Avenue is a four-way signal-controlled intersection. Oak Street runs north-south; Elm Avenue runs east-west. The intersection has dedicated left-turn lanes on Oak Street (both directions) with a protected left-turn signal phase. The speed limit on Elm Avenue approaching the intersection is 35 mph. The speed limit on Oak Street approaching the intersection is 30 mph.

Vehicle A was traveling northbound on Oak Street in the dedicated left-turn lane, executing a left turn onto westbound Elm Avenue. Vehicle B was traveling westbound on Elm Avenue through the intersection.

The point of impact was in the westbound lane of Elm Avenue, approximately 18 feet west of the center of the intersection. Debris field extended approximately 25 feet west-southwest from the point of impact. Tire marks from Vehicle B measured approximately 32 feet leading to the point of impact, consistent with hard braking.

No tire marks were observed from Vehicle A.

---

## Driver Statements

### Sarah A. Mitchell (Vehicle A)

Ms. Mitchell stated she was traveling northbound on Oak Street and entered the left-turn lane to turn onto Elm Avenue. She states she had a green left-turn arrow and proceeded to make her turn. She states she did not see Vehicle B until the moment of impact. She states Vehicle B "came out of nowhere" and must have run a red light.

### James R. Torres (Vehicle B)

Mr. Torres stated he was traveling westbound on Elm Avenue at approximately 30-35 mph. He states the traffic signal was green in his direction of travel. He states he observed Vehicle A begin its left turn and immediately applied his brakes but was unable to avoid the collision. He states Vehicle A turned directly into his path and did not yield.

## Investigating Officer's Notes

1. Both drivers claim to have had a green signal. Statements are contradictory and cannot both be accurate.
2. Witness statement partially supports Vehicle B's account but witness did not observe the signal directly prior to the collision.
3. Skid marks from Vehicle B (32 feet) suggest Vehicle B was traveling at approximately 30-35 mph prior to braking, consistent with the posted speed limit.
4. No skid marks from Vehicle A suggest Vehicle A did not brake prior to impact.
5. Point of impact location is consistent with a left-turn versus through-traffic collision.
6. Traffic signal timing data is pending from Sacramento DOT.
7. No dashcam footage was obtained from either involved vehicle.
8. A third-party dashcam from a delivery truck was identified (see supplemental report SPD-2026-031547-S1).

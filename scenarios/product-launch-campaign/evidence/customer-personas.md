# SmartDrive Target Customer Personas

**Prepared by:** Consumer Insights Team, Pinnacle Mutual Insurance
**Date:** May 2026
**Based on:** Colorado Consumer Survey (n=1,200), Focus Group Research (6 groups, 48 participants), Third-party syndicated data (J.D. Power, Mintel)

---

## Primary Persona: "Tech-Forward Traci"

### Demographics

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Age | 28-35 |
| Gender | Skews female (55%) but applicable to both |
| Income | $65,000 - $95,000 household |
| Education | College graduate |
| Location | Denver metro, Boulder, Fort Collins urban/suburban |
| Marital Status | Single or newly married, no children or 1 young child |
| Vehicle | 2-5 year old compact SUV or sedan (Subaru, Honda, Toyota, Mazda) |
| Driving Record | Clean or one minor incident in past 5 years |
| Current Insurance | National carrier (GEICO, Progressive, State Farm) |
| Annual Premium | $1,400 - $2,000 |

### Psychographics

- **Values:** Transparency, fairness, control over her financial decisions
- **Tech Behavior:** Uses 15+ apps daily; comfortable sharing data when value is clear; reads app reviews before downloading
- **Insurance Attitude:** Views insurance as a necessary commodity; shops primarily on price but considers customer reviews and claims reputation
- **Shopping Behavior:** Compares 3-4 quotes online before making a decision; reads reviews on Reddit and Google; may consult an agent for complex questions
- **Media Consumption:** Instagram (daily), YouTube (daily), podcasts (weekly), streaming TV (no cable), Google search for research
- **Brand Relationship:** Distrusts large corporations but responds to brands that feel authentic, transparent, and user-friendly

### Key Motivations

1. **Savings:** Believes she is a safe driver and resents paying the same rate as reckless drivers
2. **Control:** Wants to see exactly how her rate is calculated and what she can do to improve it
3. **Simplicity:** Prefers an app-based solution over hardware (OBD plug-in)
4. **Validation:** Likes the idea of a "safe driving score" that confirms her self-perception

### Key Barriers

1. **Privacy:** Worries about constant location tracking and phone monitoring
2. **Trust:** Skeptical that the discount is real and not a bait-and-switch
3. **Effort:** Questions whether the savings justify the hassle of switching carriers
4. **Unknown Brand:** Has never heard of Pinnacle Mutual and defaults to familiar names

### Messaging That Resonates

- "Your safe driving should save you money -- SmartDrive makes it happen"
- "No plug-in device. No rate increase. Just rewards for driving safely."
- "See your score in real time. No surprises, no fine print."
- "Your data, your control. Pause tracking anytime."

### Messaging That Falls Flat

- "Pinnacle Mutual has been trusted since 1951" (irrelevant -- she was not born)
- "Talk to your local agent today" (she wants digital-first experience)
- "Save up to 30%" without context (sounds like every other insurance ad)
- Technical jargon about telematics algorithms (she wants simplicity)

## Secondary Persona: "Family-First Fernando"

### Demographics

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Age | 35-45 |
| Gender | Skews male (52%) but applicable to both |
| Income | $85,000 - $130,000 household |
| Education | College graduate or advanced degree |
| Location | Denver suburbs, Colorado Springs, suburban Front Range |
| Marital Status | Married with 1-3 children |
| Vehicle | Mid-size SUV or minivan (Toyota Highlander, Honda Pilot, Subaru Ascent) + a second vehicle |
| Driving Record | Clean; very safety-conscious due to children in the vehicle |
| Current Insurance | Bundled home/auto with a major carrier (State Farm, Allstate, American Family) |
| Annual Premium | $2,400 - $3,600 (multi-vehicle) |
| Agent Relationship | Has an agent but rarely contacts them; would consider switching if savings are significant |

### Psychographics

- **Values:** Family safety, financial responsibility, efficiency, reliability
- **Tech Behavior:** Comfortable with technology but not an early adopter; uses apps for banking, navigation, and productivity; cautious about new app downloads
- **Insurance Attitude:** Views insurance as a financial planning tool; values coverage adequacy and claims experience; will pay more for peace of mind but expects value
- **Shopping Behavior:** Researches online but may finalize through an agent; values advice on coverage levels; bundles home and auto for discounts
- **Media Consumption:** YouTube (3-4 times/week), Facebook (daily), local news (daily), sports streaming, Google search
- **Brand Relationship:** Prefers established, stable companies; influenced by word-of-mouth from neighbors and coworkers

### Key Motivations

1. **Multi-Vehicle Savings:** Insures 2-3 vehicles and even a modest per-vehicle discount adds up significantly
2. **Family Safety:** Attracted to a product that encourages and validates safe driving habits
3. **Financial Optimization:** Actively manages household budget and looks for ways to reduce recurring costs
4. **Peace of Mind:** Wants comprehensive coverage from a carrier he trusts to pay claims

### Key Barriers

1. **Bundling Loss:** Currently gets a multi-policy discount with home + auto; switching auto-only may lose that benefit
2. **Effort:** Switching insurance for a family with multiple vehicles is time-consuming
3. **Agent Loyalty:** Has a personal relationship with his current agent and feels obligation
4. **Risk Aversion:** Hesitant to try a new product from an unfamiliar carrier for his family's primary coverage
5. **Spouse Buy-In:** Needs both partners to agree on the switch

### Messaging That Resonates

- "SmartDrive rewards the safe driving your family deserves"
- "Save across every vehicle on your policy -- safe habits add up"
- "Work with a local Colorado agent who knows SmartDrive inside and out"
- "Comprehensive coverage backed by an A-rated carrier with 75 years of experience"

### Messaging That Falls Flat

- "Download the app and get started in minutes" (feels too casual for a significant financial decision)
- "Join the telematics revolution" (does not care about tech trends)
- Price-only messaging without coverage details (he values coverage adequacy)
- Gamification features (may see driving score competition as juvenile)

## Tertiary Persona: "Budget-Conscious Blake"

### Demographics

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Age | 25-32 |
| Gender | Split evenly |
| Income | $40,000 - $65,000 individual |
| Education | Some college or associate degree |
| Location | Denver suburbs, Colorado Springs, Fort Collins |
| Marital Status | Single or cohabiting |
| Vehicle | Older model (5-10 years) sedan or hatchback |
| Driving Record | Clean or one violation in past 3 years |
| Current Insurance | Minimum coverage or liability-only with a budget carrier |
| Annual Premium | $900 - $1,400 |

### Psychographics

- **Values:** Affordability, flexibility, no long-term commitments
- **Tech Behavior:** Heavy smartphone user; downloads apps freely; less concerned about privacy if savings are immediate
- **Insurance Attitude:** Views insurance as a required expense to minimize; shops almost exclusively on price; changes carriers frequently
- **Shopping Behavior:** Comparison shops on aggregator sites (The Zebra, Policygenius); makes decisions quickly based on quoted price
- **Media Consumption:** TikTok (daily), Instagram (daily), YouTube (daily), Reddit, Google search
- **Brand Relationship:** No brand loyalty for insurance; will switch for $100/year savings

### Key Motivations

1. **Lowest Possible Price:** Primary decision factor is bottom-line cost
2. **Immediate Gratification:** Wants to see savings right away, not after 90 days
3. **Ease of Purchase:** Wants a fully digital, quick-quote experience
4. **Flexibility:** Wants month-to-month or easy cancellation options

### Key Barriers

1. **90-Day Wait:** The monitoring period before discounts apply is a significant friction point
2. **Coverage Needs:** May not want or be able to afford the coverage levels SmartDrive requires
3. **Commitment:** Resistant to installing an always-on tracking app
4. **Attention Span:** Will leave the quote funnel if it takes more than 3 minutes

### Messaging That Resonates

- "Safe drivers save -- it's that simple"
- "No plug-in, no hassle. Just drive."
- "Get a quote in under 2 minutes"

### Messaging That Falls Flat

- Anything about comprehensive coverage or financial planning
- Long-form content or detailed product explanations
- Agent-focused messaging (wants pure digital)
- Messaging about Pinnacle's history or stability (does not care)

## Persona Prioritization Matrix

| Persona | Volume Potential | Premium Value | Retention Likelihood | Strategic Priority |
|---------|-----------------|---------------|---------------------|--------------------|
| Tech-Forward Traci | High | Medium | Medium-High | **Primary** |
| Family-First Fernando | Medium | High | High | **Primary** |
| Budget-Conscious Blake | High | Low | Low | **Secondary** |

**Recommendation:** Focus primary marketing efforts on Tech-Forward Traci and Family-First Fernando. These personas represent the highest combination of premium value and retention likelihood. Budget-Conscious Blake can be addressed through lower-cost digital channels (social media, search) but should not be the primary focus of the $500K budget.

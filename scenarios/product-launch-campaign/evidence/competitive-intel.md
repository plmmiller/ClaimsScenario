# Competitive Intelligence Report - Colorado UBI Market

**Prepared by:** Strategic Planning & Competitive Intelligence
**Date:** May 2026
**Classification:** Confidential - Internal Use Only

## Competitive Landscape Summary

The Colorado auto insurance market features four primary UBI competitors and two major traditional carriers whose brand presence and pricing power affect SmartDrive's entry strategy. This report profiles each competitor's UBI product, marketing approach, pricing position, and agent strategy.

---

## Competitor 1: Progressive - Snapshot

### Product Overview

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Product Name | Snapshot |
| Technology | OBD plug-in device OR smartphone app (customer chooses) |
| Monitoring Period | 6 months |
| Maximum Discount | 30% |
| Rate Increase Possible | No (discount only) |
| Colorado Market Share (UBI) | Estimated 35% of UBI policies |
| National Brand Awareness | 92% (highest in industry) |

### Marketing Strategy

- **Annual U.S. Ad Spend:** Approximately $2.2 billion (all products)
- **Colorado-Specific Spend:** Estimated $8-12M annually on auto insurance advertising
- **Primary Channels:** Television (Flo campaign), digital (search + social), streaming audio
- **Messaging Focus:** Savings, simplicity, name-your-price tool, bundling discounts
- **UBI-Specific Messaging:** "Snapshot can save you up to 30%. Plug in and save."
- **Tone:** Humorous, consumer-friendly, mass-market appeal

### Strengths

1. Massive brand awareness and advertising budget that SmartDrive cannot match
2. Dual technology option (OBD or app) gives consumers choice
3. Established agent and direct-to-consumer channels
4. Deep data and actuarial experience with UBI pricing
5. Name-your-price tool is a strong lead generation mechanism

### Weaknesses

1. OBD plug-in option is perceived as outdated and inconvenient by younger drivers
2. 6-month monitoring period is longer than most competitors
3. Agent commission structure for Snapshot is not differentiated from standard auto
4. Privacy messaging is minimal -- focused more on savings than data protection
5. Snapshot is not positioned as a premium or tech-forward product

### Implications for SmartDrive

- Cannot compete on brand awareness or advertising budget
- Differentiate on app-only technology (no plug-in), shorter monitoring period (90 days vs. 6 months), and transparent real-time scoring
- Target consumers who find Progressive's approach "old school" or who want more control over their data

## Competitor 2: Root Insurance

### Product Overview

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Product Name | Root Auto Insurance |
| Technology | Smartphone app only |
| Monitoring Period | 2-4 weeks (test drive) |
| Maximum Discount | Up to 52% (claimed) |
| Rate Increase Possible | Yes -- poor drivers may receive higher-than-average rates or be declined |
| Colorado Market Share (total auto) | 2.1% and growing |
| Colorado Market Share (UBI) | Estimated 28% of UBI policies |
| Founded | 2015 (Columbus, OH) |

### Marketing Strategy

- **Estimated National Digital Ad Spend:** $36M/year (heavy digital, no TV)
- **Colorado Digital Spend:** Estimated $1.5-2M annually
- **Primary Channels:** Facebook, Instagram, Google Ads, TikTok, podcast sponsorships, app store optimization
- **Messaging Focus:** "Fair insurance based on how you drive, not who you are." Anti-traditional-insurance positioning.
- **Tone:** Disruptive, millennial-focused, tech-forward, anti-establishment
- **Differentiator:** Short test-drive period, direct-to-consumer (no agents), fully mobile experience

### Strengths

1. Short test-drive period (2-4 weeks) versus SmartDrive's 90 days -- significantly lower friction
2. Strong brand resonance with 25-35 demographic who distrust traditional insurers
3. Fully digital experience with no agent overhead, enabling aggressive pricing
4. Effective social media presence with high engagement rates
5. App Store rating: 4.7 stars (high consumer satisfaction with app)
6. Aggressive pricing for best-risk drivers (lowest sticker price in market)

### Weaknesses

1. **Can increase rates:** Root can charge higher rates for poor drivers -- SmartDrive's "no increase" guarantee is a differentiator
2. Financial instability: Root has never been profitable; reported $280M net loss in 2025
3. No agent channel: consumers who want personal advice must go elsewhere
4. Claims service complaints: Colorado Division of Insurance received 47 complaints in 2025 (above average per policy)
5. Aggressive underwriting: declines a significant percentage of applicants after the test drive
6. Privacy concerns: recent news coverage of telematics data sharing practices
7. No bundling options (auto only, no home/renters)

### Implications for SmartDrive

- Root is the primary UBI competitor for the 25-35 demographic
- SmartDrive's "no rate increase" guarantee directly counters Root's pricing model
- Agent channel and Pinnacle's financial stability are meaningful differentiators
- Root's claims service issues and privacy concerns are exploitable weaknesses (in agent conversations, not public advertising)
- The 90-day monitoring period versus Root's 2-4 week test drive is a significant disadvantage that must be addressed in messaging

## Competitor 3: Allstate - Drivewise

### Product Overview

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Product Name | Drivewise |
| Technology | Smartphone app |
| Monitoring Period | 6 months |
| Maximum Discount | 25% |
| Rate Increase Possible | No |
| Colorado Market Share (UBI) | Estimated 18% of UBI policies |

### Marketing Strategy

- **Ad Spend:** Part of Allstate's $1.8B national ad budget (Mayhem campaign)
- **UBI-Specific Marketing:** Relatively low emphasis; Drivewise is a feature, not a standalone product
- **Channels:** TV, digital, agent referral
- **Messaging:** "Safe driving pays off with Drivewise rewards"
- **Tone:** Protective, family-oriented, established

### Strengths

1. Strong brand trust and recognition (Dennis Haysbert/Mayhem campaigns)
2. Extensive agent network in Colorado
3. Bundling discounts (home + auto) that create switching barriers
4. No rate increase model (same as SmartDrive)
5. Established claims service infrastructure

### Weaknesses

1. Drivewise receives minimal marketing investment compared to core brand
2. Maximum 25% discount is lower than competitors
3. 6-month monitoring period is longest in market
4. App experience rated lower than Root (3.9 stars vs. 4.7 stars)
5. Perceived as a traditional insurer, not a tech innovator

### Implications for SmartDrive

- Allstate is not aggressively marketing Drivewise, creating opportunity
- SmartDrive can position as a more modern, tech-forward UBI option with better app experience
- Target Allstate customers who are interested in UBI but underwhelmed by Drivewise

## Traditional Carrier Threats

### State Farm (18.3% Colorado market share)

- **Drive Safe & Save:** UBI program using OnStar, connected car, or app; up to 30% discount
- **Strength:** Largest agent network in Colorado (estimated 180+ agents), dominant brand, bundling power
- **Weakness:** UBI product not heavily promoted; agent network is captive (cannot sell SmartDrive)
- **Threat Level:** HIGH for overall market competition; MEDIUM for UBI specifically

### GEICO (14.7% Colorado market share)

- **No UBI product** currently offered in Colorado
- **Strength:** Lowest base rates for many demographics; massive brand awareness; direct-to-consumer efficiency
- **Weakness:** No agent support; no UBI offering; no bundling in many states
- **Threat Level:** HIGH for price-sensitive consumers; LOW for UBI-interested consumers

| Message Theme | Progressive | Root | Allstate | SmartDrive (Proposed) |
|---------------|------------|------|----------|----------------------|
| Price | "Name your price" | "Fair rates based on how you drive" | "Bundle and save" | "Safe drivers save up to 30%" |

### Key Competitive Differentiators for SmartDrive

1. **Privacy-first positioning:** No major competitor emphasizes data privacy. SmartDrive can own this space.
2. **No rate increase guarantee:** Directly counters Root's model where rates can increase.
3. **Agent + digital hybrid:** Only UBI product with both strong digital experience and local agent support.
4. **Transparent scoring:** Real-time score visibility with factor-level detail.
5. **90-day (not 6-month) monitoring:** Faster than Progressive/Allstate/Nationwide (but slower than Root).
6. **Privacy Mode:** Unique feature allowing consumers to pause tracking without penalty.

## Recommended Competitive Strategy

### Against Progressive Snapshot
- Position as the modern, app-only alternative to Snapshot's "outdated plug-in device"
- Emphasize 90-day versus 6-month monitoring period
- Target consumers who find Progressive's mass-market approach impersonal

### Against Root Insurance
- Emphasize financial stability (Pinnacle's A- rating vs. Root's unprofitability)
- Highlight the "no rate increase" guarantee (Root can and does increase rates)
- Position local agent support as a value-add for consumers who want guidance
- Address Root's claims service issues through agent conversations (not advertising)

### Against Allstate Drivewise
- Position SmartDrive as the dedicated UBI product (versus Drivewise as an afterthought)
- Emphasize higher max discount (30% vs. 25%) and shorter monitoring period
- Target Allstate customers who are interested in UBI but underwhelmed by Drivewise

### Against State Farm / GEICO (Traditional)
- Avoid direct price competition (cannot win on base rate or advertising budget)
- Position UBI as a smarter way to pay for insurance that rewards individual behavior
- Target consumers who feel they subsidize bad drivers under traditional pricing models

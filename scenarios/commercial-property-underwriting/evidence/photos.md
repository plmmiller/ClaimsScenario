Close-up of one of the two original roof drains. The drain bowl is clear of debris. The surrounding membrane is properly adhered to the drain flange. No signs of ponding or membrane deterioration around the drain.

**Photo 10 - Roof Drain (Supplemental)**
Close-up of one of the two supplemental roof drains installed after the 2023 water damage claim. The drain is in good condition and appears properly installed. The membrane tie-in to the new drain flange shows clean workmanship.

**Photo 11 - HVAC Unit on Roof**
Close-up of one of the four Carrier WeatherExpert rooftop HVAC units. The unit appears in good condition with no visible corrosion or damage. The roof curb is properly sealed to the membrane. Ductwork penetrations are flashed and sealed.

**Photo 12 - Roof Edge and Coping**
View of the roof edge and metal coping at the south (front) parapet. The coping is intact and properly sealed. No visible gaps or separations at the coping joints. Drip edge appears functional.

### Interior Views - Storage Area

**Photo 17 - Storage Room (Wide Shot)**
The rear storage area (approximately 3,000 sq ft). Metal pallet racking and shelving units hold boxed inventory. Storage appears organized with clear aisle space maintained. Sprinkler clearance appears adequate (18+ inches below deflectors observed). No storage directly against electrical panels.

**Photo 18 - Water-Stained Ceiling Tiles**
Two ceiling tiles in the rear storage area showing faint brown water staining. The staining pattern is consistent with water intrusion from above. The tiles are dry to the touch. Moisture meter reading at time of inspection: 8% (normal range). These tiles have not been replaced since the June 2023 water damage event. The staining appears historical, not active.

**Photo 19 - Storage Room Sprinkler Coverage**
Close-up of sprinkler heads in the storage room. Heads are pendent-type, spaced at approximately 100 sq ft per head (consistent with Ordinary Hazard Group 1 design). No obstructions observed below any heads. Temperature rating labels visible (165 degrees F).

### Interior Views - Repair Area

**Photo 20 - Repair Area Overview**
The dedicated repair area (approximately 800 sq ft) in the northeast corner of the premises. Three workbench stations are visible, each with task lighting, magnification lamps, and ESD mats. The area is separated from the retail floor by a half-wall and glass partition.

**Photo 21 - Soldering Station Close-up**
Close-up of one of three soldering stations. A temperature-controlled soldering iron (Hakko FX-888D) is mounted in its holder. Fume extraction arm is positioned above the work surface. The work surface is ESD-safe laminate. Small parts bins are organized on a pegboard above the station. No combustible materials observed within arm's reach of the soldering station.

**Photo 22 - Heat Gun Station**
A heat gun (Wagner HT3500) stored in a wall-mounted holder at one of the workbench stations. The heat gun is used for adhesive removal and component rework per the store manager. A ceramic heat mat is positioned on the work surface beneath the heat gun's operating area.

**Photo 23 - Repair Area Ventilation**
The exhaust ventilation system serving the repair area. A ceiling-mounted exhaust fan with flexible ductwork connects to a wall-penetration exhaust port. The fan appeared operational during the inspection (confirmed running). Individual fume extraction arms at each soldering station feed into the main exhaust system.

**Photo 24 - Repair Area Fire Protection**
Two fire extinguishers mounted on the wall in the repair area: one ABC dry chemical (10 lb) and one CO2 (15 lb). Both have current inspection tags (February 2026). A rate-of-rise heat detector is mounted on the ceiling above the workbench area. Sprinkler head visible at the standard ceiling spacing.

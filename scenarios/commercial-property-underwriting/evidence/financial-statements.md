- Total loss (complete destruction requiring relocation): 12-18 months

**Extended Period of Indemnity:**
The applicant requests a 12-month extended period of indemnity to cover the ramp-up period after restoration of operations. Given the specialized nature of the retail electronics business and the time required to rebuild customer traffic after a loss, a 12-month EPI is reasonable.

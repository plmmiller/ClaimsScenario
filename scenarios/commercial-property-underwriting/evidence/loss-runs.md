#### Loss Description
During a severe thunderstorm on June 15, 2023, a clogged roof drain on the flat roof caused water to pond on the roof surface above the rear storage and display area of the insured premises. Water intruded through the roof membrane at a seam point near the clogged drain, affecting approximately 600 square feet of ceiling area. Water damaged suspended ceiling tiles, drywall, and commercial carpet in the affected area. Approximately $30,000 in electronic inventory (primarily laptops and tablets stored on shelving below the leak point) sustained water damage and was deemed a total loss. The insured reported the loss the next business day and a field adjuster inspected the premises on June 18, 2023.

#### Adjuster Notes
- Root cause: Clogged roof drain caused ponding on flat roof. Drain was blocked by accumulated debris (leaves, granules from aging roof membrane).
- The roof membrane was original (22 years old at time of loss) and showed signs of wear at the seam where water intruded.
- Insured took immediate mitigation action: placed tarps, moved remaining inventory, engaged water extraction contractor.
- Mitigation response was timely and appropriate. No mold development.
- Recommendation: Roof replacement and installation of additional drainage. Insured should implement quarterly roof drain cleaning schedule.

#### Post-Loss Corrective Actions (Documented)
1. Two additional roof drains installed (August 2023) - Cost: $3,200 (insured's expense)
2. Full roof replacement completed (October 2021 - NOTE: This was prior to the loss. The 2021 roof replacement predates the 2023 claim. Upon further review, the 2023 loss occurred on the section of roof that was replaced in 2021.)
3. **Correction:** The roof was fully replaced in 2021 as part of the $85,000 renovation. The 2023 water intrusion occurred on the new roof at a drain point that became clogged. The root cause was drainage maintenance, not roof condition.
4. Quarterly roof drain cleaning schedule implemented (verified by building management)
5. Inventory storage relocated away from areas directly below roof penetrations

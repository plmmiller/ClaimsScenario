## Footage Overview

The dashcam footage was recorded from the front-facing camera of a delivery truck that was stopped at the intersection of Oak Street and Elm Avenue, facing southbound on Oak Street. The truck was stopped at the red light on Oak Street's southbound approach to the intersection.

**Total Relevant Footage Duration:** Approximately 18 seconds (from 14:46:42 to 14:47:00)

The footage cuts out abruptly at approximately the 14:47:00 timestamp -- approximately 1 second before the point of impact -- due to what appears to be a momentary camera power interruption. Mr. Haines reports the dashcam has an intermittent wiring issue and occasionally loses power briefly when the truck's engine idles at low RPM. The camera restarted approximately 8 seconds after the collision had already occurred.

## Technical Analysis Notes

### What the footage establishes:
1. The traffic signal facing southbound Oak Street was RED throughout the visible footage, which means the signal for at least one other phase (Elm Avenue through-traffic and/or Oak Street left-turn arrow) should have been GREEN.
2. Vehicle A initiated and committed to the left turn during the captured timeframe.
3. Vehicle B entered the intersection from the east, traveling westbound on Elm Avenue.
4. Vehicle B applied brakes approximately 2-3 seconds before the footage cut out.
5. Vehicle A did not appear to brake or take evasive action during the captured portion.
6. The approximate speeds appear consistent with posted limits for both vehicles.

### What the footage does NOT establish:
1. **The footage does not capture the actual moment of impact** due to the camera power interruption.
2. **The footage cannot confirm whether Vehicle A had a protected green left-turn arrow.** The northbound left-turn signal faces away from the camera. It is possible that:
   - (a) Vehicle A had a protected green arrow and Vehicle B entered on a red or late yellow, OR
   - (b) The signal had transitioned to the Elm Avenue green phase and Vehicle A turned on a solid green (unprotected left turn) failing to yield to oncoming traffic, OR
   - (c) Vehicle A was completing a turn that began on a protected arrow that had since expired.
3. **The exact speed of either vehicle** cannot be precisely calculated from this footage alone without professional accident reconstruction analysis, though visual estimates are consistent with posted limits.
4. **Whether Vehicle B's traffic signal was green, yellow, or red** at the moment Vehicle B entered the intersection -- the Elm Avenue signals are not directly visible from the dashcam angle.

### Signal Phase Analysis
The intersection uses a 3-phase signal:
- **Phase 1:** Oak Street protected left turn (green arrow for both NB and SB left turns; all other directions red)
- **Phase 2:** Oak Street through traffic (solid green for NB and SB through; Elm Ave red)
- **Phase 3:** Elm Avenue through traffic (solid green for EB and WB; Oak St red)

Since the southbound Oak Street signal was red, the intersection was in either Phase 1 (protected left-turn) or Phase 3 (Elm Avenue green). This is the critical ambiguity: if Phase 1, Vehicle A had the right of way with a green arrow; if Phase 3, Vehicle B had the right of way with a green light and Vehicle A failed to yield.

The footage does not resolve which phase was active.

## Dashcam Owner Statement

**Robert Haines, Driver - Valley Fresh Produce, Inc.**

"I was stopped at the red light on Oak Street heading south. I was waiting for the light to change so I could go through the intersection. I saw the silver car start to turn left across in front of me, from the other side of the intersection. Then I saw the blue car coming from my left on Elm. I thought they were going to hit and then I heard the crash. By the time I realized what happened, it was already over. I pulled over and called it in and then the cop asked me about my dashcam. I told him it cuts out sometimes -- the wiring on the power cable is loose and I've been meaning to fix it. Bad luck that it cut out right at that moment."

**Q: Did you see what the traffic signals were showing for the other directions?**
"I only saw my light, which was red. I wasn't really paying attention to what the other signals were doing. I think I saw a green arrow for the left turn at some point, but honestly I can't say for sure. I was looking at my phone for a second -- don't tell my boss -- and then the next thing I was hearing the crash."

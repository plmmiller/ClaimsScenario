## Valuation Methodology

This appraisal uses the segregated cost method to estimate the replacement cost new (RCN) of the building structure and tenant improvements. Replacement cost represents the cost to reconstruct the building with materials of like kind and quality at current market prices, without deduction for depreciation. Costs are based on Marshall Valuation Service (MVS) construction cost data adjusted for the Austin, TX market (locality multiplier: 0.97) and current material/labor cost indices as of Q1 2026.

## Adequacy of Requested Coverage

### Building Coverage

| | Applicant Request | Appraised Value | Adequacy |
|--|-------------------|-----------------|----------|
| Building (TIB only) | $350,000 | $314,900 | Adequate |
| Building (full structure) | $1,200,000 | $1,575,000 | **Inadequate** - 76% of RCN |
| **Recommended Building Limit** | | **$1,575,000** | |

**Coinsurance Note:** At the requested $1,200,000 building limit with 80% coinsurance, the insured would be subject to a coinsurance penalty on any partial loss. The 80% coinsurance requirement is $1,260,000 (80% of $1,575,000). The requested limit of $1,200,000 falls short of the coinsurance requirement by $60,000, resulting in a coinsurance penalty of approximately 4.8% on any partial loss.

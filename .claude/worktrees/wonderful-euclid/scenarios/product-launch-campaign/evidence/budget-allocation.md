### Email Sequences Planned

1. **Welcome Series (5 emails):** Introduces SmartDrive, explains UBI, addresses privacy, drives to quote
2. **Nurture Sequence (8 emails):** Educational content about safe driving, savings stories, app features
3. **Agent Introduction (3 emails):** Introduces local agent, provides contact info, drives to consultation
4. **Launch Announcement (1 email):** Product availability notification to opted-in prospects
5. **Post-Quote Follow-up (4 emails):** Re-engagement for users who started but did not complete a quote
6. **Customer Onboarding (5 emails):** App setup, driving tips, score explanation, Day 91 milestone

## Budget Governance

1. **Monthly Review:** Budget vs. actual reviewed monthly by marketing team and VP Marketing
2. **Reallocation Authority:** Marketing manager may reallocate up to 10% between line items without VP approval; larger shifts require VP sign-off
3. **Contingency Use:** The $20,000 contingency requires VP Marketing approval and documented justification
4. **Invoicing:** All vendor invoices submitted within 30 days; net 30 payment terms
5. **ROI Reporting:** Channel-level ROI calculated monthly; full campaign ROI at end of campaign period

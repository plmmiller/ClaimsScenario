## Brand Overview

### Mission Statement

Pinnacle Mutual Insurance protects what matters most to the families and communities we serve, combining 75 years of trusted expertise with innovative solutions for today's world.

### Brand Promise

We promise to treat every customer like a neighbor -- with honesty, fairness, and the personal attention that only a mutual company can provide.

### Brand Values

1. **Trust:** We earn trust through transparency, fair dealing, and keeping our promises
2. **Innovation:** We embrace new technologies and ideas that make insurance simpler and more personal
3. **Community:** We are invested in the communities we serve and the people who live in them
4. **Expertise:** We combine deep insurance knowledge with modern tools to deliver the best solutions
5. **Mutuality:** As a mutual company, our customers are our owners. Their interests come first.

## Brand Voice

### Tone Attributes

| Attribute | What This Means | What This Does NOT Mean |
|-----------|----------------|------------------------|
| **Confident** | We speak with authority earned through 75 years of experience | We are not arrogant or condescending |
| **Clear** | We use plain language that anyone can understand | We do not use insurance jargon without explanation |
| **Warm** | We sound like a knowledgeable friend, not a faceless corporation | We are not overly casual or unprofessional |
| **Honest** | We say what we mean and set accurate expectations | We do not exaggerate, mislead, or use fine print to contradict headlines |
| **Forward-Looking** | We embrace innovation and talk about the future positively | We do not dismiss tradition or dismiss customers who prefer the status quo |

### Writing Guidelines

1. **Lead with the benefit, not the feature.** Say "Save money for driving safely" not "Our telematics algorithm calculates a Driving Score."

2. **Use second person (you/your).** "Your safe driving should save you money" not "Customers who drive safely may be eligible for discounts."

3. **Avoid superlatives unless substantiated.** Do not say "the best" or "the most innovative" without third-party validation. Say "designed to reward" or "built to save."

4. **Explain the why, not just the what.** "We use a smartphone app instead of a plug-in device because it's simpler and does not drain your car's battery" not "SmartDrive uses a smartphone app."

5. **Acknowledge concerns honestly.** When addressing privacy: "We understand you're trusting us with your driving data. Here's exactly what we collect, how we use it, and how you stay in control" not "Your data is safe with us."

6. **Localize for Colorado.** Reference Colorado-specific elements: Rocky Mountain driving, Denver commutes, Colorado weather, local landmarks. Avoid generic stock imagery.

### Messaging Hierarchy

#### Primary Message
"SmartDrive rewards your safe driving with real savings -- because what you do behind the wheel should determine what you pay."

#### Secondary Messages
1. **Privacy:** "Your data, your control. See exactly what we collect, pause tracking anytime, and delete your data when you leave."
2. **Savings:** "Safe drivers save up to 30%. And your rate never goes up based on your SmartDrive score -- that's our guarantee."
3. **Simplicity:** "No plug-in device, no complicated setup. Download the app, drive safely, and save."
4. **Trust:** "Backed by Pinnacle Mutual -- 75 years of protecting what matters most, now serving Colorado."
5. **Agent Support:** "Questions? Your local Colorado agent is here to help, not a chatbot."

## Regulatory Compliance for Advertising

### Required Elements (Colorado Division of Insurance)

All consumer-facing insurance advertisements must include:

1. **Full legal company name:** "Pinnacle Mutual Insurance Company"
2. **NAIC number:** #48291
3. **State of domicile:** "Domiciled in Missouri"
4. **Disclaimer on discount claims:** "Discount amount varies based on individual driving behavior. Maximum discount of 30% available on SmartDrive Elite tier only. Average discount is 12-15%. Safe driving discount applies after 90-day monitoring period."
5. **Not a guarantee language:** "Savings are not guaranteed and depend on individual driving behavior as measured by the SmartDrive app."

### Required Disclosures for Telematics Advertising

Per Colorado SB 21-169, telematics advertisements must disclose:
- The types of driving data collected
- That participation is voluntary
- How to access the company's full telematics privacy policy

### Prohibited Claims

1. Do NOT guarantee specific savings amounts (use "up to" with average disclosure)
2. Do NOT claim SmartDrive is "the cheapest" or "the lowest rate" without substantiation
3. Do NOT imply that telematics data will never be used in underwriting decisions beyond the discount
4. Do NOT use testimonials without signed release forms and FTC compliance
5. Do NOT compare rates to specific competitors by name in advertising (acceptable in agent sales materials only)

## Photography & Imagery Guidelines

### Style

- **Authentic, not stock:** Use real Colorado settings, real people, genuine moments
- **Diverse and inclusive:** Represent the diversity of Colorado's population in age, ethnicity, family structure, and lifestyle
- **Active and positive:** Show people living their lives -- driving through mountain passes, picking up kids, commuting through Denver -- not sitting at desks reading policies
- **Colorado-specific:** Use recognizable Colorado landscapes (Flatirons, Red Rocks, I-70 mountain corridor, Denver skyline) to establish local connection
- **Technology integration:** Show the SmartDrive app naturally in context (glanced at before driving, reviewed at home) -- never while driving

### Do Not

- Use stock photos that feel generic or could be any state
- Show people using phones while driving
- Use images that feel overly corporate or staged
- Feature luxury vehicles or lifestyles that feel aspirational rather than relatable
- Use images with competing brand logos visible

## SmartDrive Sub-Brand Guidelines

SmartDrive operates as a product sub-brand under the Pinnacle Mutual parent brand. It uses the SmartDrive wordmark alongside the Pinnacle Mutual logo.

### SmartDrive Logo Usage

- Always pair the SmartDrive wordmark with "by Pinnacle Mutual" in smaller type below
- The SmartDrive Teal (#00B4D8) may be used as the primary accent color in SmartDrive-specific materials
- Parent brand colors (Pinnacle Navy, Summit Gold) should remain present to maintain brand connection
- SmartDrive materials should feel more modern and tech-forward than general Pinnacle materials while staying within the brand system

### App Interface Alignment

- All marketing screenshots and mockups of the SmartDrive app must accurately represent the actual app interface
- Do not create idealized or aspirational app mockups that differ from the production app
- Driving Score displays in marketing materials must use the correct color coding (Green/Orange/Red) matching the app
